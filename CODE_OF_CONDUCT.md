# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not
accepted, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[YuraZakaryan](https://github.com/YuraZakaryan), through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban
participants, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
